About Kabale Hospital

Serving south-western Uganda with trained staff working around the clock.

Our story

Kabale Hospital serves patients from Kabale and neighbouring districts, offering general, specialist and emergency care in one place.

Our mission

To provide safe, timely and respectful healthcare to every patient.

Our vision

A healthy, productive community in the Kigezi region.

Our values

  • Compassion for every patient
  • Integrity and accountability
  • Teamwork across departments
  • Respect for privacy and dignity
  • Continuous learning and improvement

Our team

Doctors, clinical officers, nurses, midwives, laboratory technicians, pharmacists and support staff.

Departments

Outpatient (OPD) Mon to Sat

General consultation, minor treatment and referrals. Monday to Friday 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, Saturday 8:00 am to 1:00 pm.

Emergency and casualty 24 hours

Open every day, all day. Triage nurses assess each patient on arrival.

Maternity 24 hours

Antenatal clinic, labour ward and postnatal care for mothers and babies.

Paediatrics Mon to Fri

Children's clinic and ward, including immunisation days.

Laboratory and imaging Mon to Sat

Diagnostic tests and scans requested by clinicians.

Pharmacy Mon to Sat

Dispensing of prescribed medicines.

Surgery By schedule

Theatre lists and surgical clinics. Emergency surgery is available at any time.

Dental and eye care Clinic days

Routine treatment, screening and referrals.
